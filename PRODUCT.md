# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS + Three.js (offline-first Progressive Web App and packaged Android WebView APK via local Gradle/SDK build pipeline).

## Users

Individuals seeking structured self-discipline who find standard checklist habit trackers unmotivating or easy to ignore. They use HabitOps on their phone or desktop throughout the day (morning, afternoon, and evening execution windows) to lock in physical, cognitive, and lifestyle habits with tangible stakes.

## Product Purpose

HabitOps turns daily routines into tactical combat encounters against weekly boss entities. Success means consistent daily execution of habits, sustained multi-day streaks, defeated weekly bosses, and long-term progression toward Level 100 Apex Grandmaster without cloud tracking or subscription friction.

## Positioning

Unlike passive habit checklists where tapping a checkbox has no friction or consequence, HabitOps requires a deliberate 2-second press-and-hold strike to execute a habit (preventing accidental taps), rolls real-time critical hit multipliers against a boss health bar, and penalizes missed days by letting the boss regenerate 15% HP per day and enter an Enraged state (+10% Max HP) when healed to full capacity.

## Operating Context

Used daily across morning, afternoon, and evening time windows on Android phones and desktop browsers. Operates 100% offline using local storage (`localStorage`) with one-click JSON backup/restore and procedural WebAudio synthesis (zero external MP3 dependencies).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 2-second hold-to-clear strike verification with rising procedural WebAudio synthesizer charge and particle burst feedback.
- Weekly boss combat encounters (*The Procrastination Titan*, *The Feed Phantom*, *The Dopamine Leech*) with 15%/day inactivity health regeneration and +10% Max HP enrage penalty.
- Unlimited level scaling with 9 military prestige rank titles capping at Level 100 (*Apex Grandmaster*).
- 5-tier stacked XP multiplier engine (Intensity Rank, Streak Momentum, Daily Chain Combo, Prime Window, Critical Strike Roll).
- Streak Freeze Shield (*Tactical EMP*) earned every 10 levels to absorb missed-day boss regeneration.
- Period-grouped non-spam notifications (Morning, Afternoon, Evening briefings).
- Sharable Operative Battle Card PNG generator in the profile dossier.
- Strict 0px square-edge geometry (`border-radius: 0px`) across the core application HUD.

## Brand Commitments

- Commercial Name: **HabitOps** (strictly styled as **HabitOps** across all user-facing interfaces, documentation, application headers, Android package metadata, and commercial materials).
- Commercial Website Aesthetic: Crisp Daylight Tactical light theme (Warm Alabaster/Stone `#F3F4F0` ground, Architectural Slate `#E6E8E2` surface step, Deep Carbon `#111315` ink, Signal Vermilion `#B81420` accent), monumental `General Sans` display typography, interactive Three.js 3D Strike Core, and radical simplicity free of AI-generated design tells.
- Application HUD Aesthetic: High-contrast military tactical command center with strict 0px square-edge geometry (`border-radius: 0px`), procedural WebAudio audio feedback, and anti-slip 2.0s hold verification.
- Separation of Concerns: The public commercial site (`landing.html`) remains clean and customer-focused; creator production tools (Google Flow video/image prompt deck and downloadable `.wav` WebAudio soundboard) live on a dedicated studio route (`flow-studio.html`).

## Evidence on Hand

- Live working application (`index.html`, `js/app.js`, `js/state.js`, `js/audio.js`, `css/styles.css`).
- Signed downloadable Android package (`habitops.apk`).
- Promotional visual slots and flagship commercial video player ready for Google Flow renders.
- No third-party customer testimonials, press quotes, or user count claims may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. **Deliberate Friction Over Passive Taps:** Every habit check-in is a physical 2-second hold strike with immediate acoustic and visual weight.
2. **Real Consequences for Inaction:** Skipping days heals the adversary, making discipline visibly matter.
3. **Zero Bloat, Total Sovereignty:** Offline-first architecture, procedural WebAudio synthesis, and local data ownership with no accounts or trackers.
4. **Uncluttered Clarity:** The commercial storefront shows the product and its core mechanism directly without filler sections or decorative clutter.
