# Surface Brief: landing.html

- **Scope:** Public commercial storefront (`landing.html`)
- **Mode:** Persuade
- **Audience & Action:** Individuals looking for a high-accountability habit tracker who will test the 2-second hold strike, watch the flagship film, and download `habitops.apk` or launch the web app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The name HABITOPS stands as a monumental typographic structure paired with an interactive Three.js 3D Strike Core that physically demonstrates the 2-second hold-to-clear mechanism, refusing standard SaaS card grids, dark-neon AI clichés, and decorative eyebrow labels.
