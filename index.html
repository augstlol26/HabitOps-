HabitOps Commercial Compliance & Privacy

1. 100% Local-First Privacy Architecture

HabitOps stores your protocols, XP progression, custom profile photos, and session telemetry strictly on your device (`localStorage` & offline cache). We never sell, track, or transmit your personal habits to third-party ad networks.

You retain full ownership of your data. Use the Backup JSON and Restore Backup controls in your Profile at any time to migrate your progress across phones or desktops, or export a CSV spreadsheet for external analysis.

HabitOps is a gamified behavioral productivity system. Physical training, cold exposure, and fasting protocols are voluntary self-improvement goals; consult a physician before beginning intense physical regimens.
